ALBERT CANAL STAGE MODEL (for the 3D illusion projection)
Scale 1:1600. Whole model 720 x 404.8 mm in eight tiles, same layout as the detailed model (see ../tile_layout.png).

This is a clean white stage: the canal, both water levels, the quay, the swap station platform and the lock walls and islands.
Nothing else is printed. Vessels, cranes, containers, buildings, trees and the road bridge are all projected,
drawn so they stand up in 3D for someone looking from the canal (north) side.

FILES
stage_A1 to stage_A4   north row, west to east
stage_B1 to stage_B4   south row, west to east

PRINT SETTINGS (Bambu Studio, P2S, 0.4 mm nozzle)
Matte white PLA, 0.16 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, 5 top layers, no supports, no brim.
Print each tile flat as it opens. Do not rotate or scale.
For the smoothest projection surface, turn on ironing for the top surfaces.

ASSEMBLY
Glue the tiles to a flat board. Fill and sand the joins if you want them invisible under the projection.
