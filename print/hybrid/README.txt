ALBERT CANAL MODEL, IN-BETWEEN VERSION (real 3D plus the projected 3D illusion)
Scale 1:1600. Whole model 720 x 404.8 mm in eight tiles, same layout as the other versions (see ../tile_layout.png).

PRINTED (solid things that never move)
Terrain, both water levels, quay, the battery swapping station (platform, docking stations, battery storage, canopy, control building),
the lock with its walls, islands, control tower, fixed gates and gate towers, the road bridge, all buildings with their roofs, the container stacks.

PROJECTED (things that move, or are too thin to print well)
Vessels, the e-barge, the tender, battery packs, the gantry cranes, trucks, trees, water, and all colour.

The projection knows the height of every printed part, so colours land on the right roofs and container tops,
while the projected vessels and cranes are drawn to stand up in 3D for a viewer on the canal (north) side.

FILES
hybrid_A1 to hybrid_A4   north row, west to east
hybrid_B1 to hybrid_B4   south row, west to east

PRINT SETTINGS (Bambu Studio, P2S, 0.4 mm nozzle)
Matte white PLA. 0.12 mm layers for A3 and A4 (lock), 0.16 mm for the rest. 3 walls, 15% infill, 5 top layers, no supports, no brim.
Tile A3 has the road bridge as a real span over the three lock channels. Leave supports off, the printer bridges it.
Print each tile flat as it opens. Do not rotate or scale.
